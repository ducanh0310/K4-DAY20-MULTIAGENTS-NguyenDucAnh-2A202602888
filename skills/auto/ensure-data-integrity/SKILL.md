---
name: ensure-data-integrity
description: Use this skill to validate and clean data before processing it.
---
- Always check for missing or null values in your datasets and handle them appropriately (e.g., imputation, removal).
- Ensure that data types are consistent and correctly formatted (e.g., dates, currency).
- Remove duplicate entries from datasets to maintain data integrity and accuracy.
- Validate that all required fields are present in the dataset before proceeding with analysis.
- Standardize categorical data (e.g., region names) to ensure consistency across the dataset.
- Document any data cleaning steps taken to maintain transparency and reproducibility in your analysis.
